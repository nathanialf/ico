## 2. The whole match spec (~lines 115 to 166)

The old spec is retired, not just its tooling. Replace everything from
`**Match-effort rule: a 30-iteration STALL is required before` down to and
including the end of the `**Hard / plateaued functions` paragraph
(`... live in \`docs/COOKBOOK.md\` §13.`) with the single paragraph below.
That removes the 30-iteration stall gate, the `match_loop.py next` verdict,
the cracked-at-iteration anecdotes, the permuter-after-the-stall sentence and
the `decomp-convergence` / `decomp-match` skill framing in one edit.

**NEW**

```
**How matching runs now.** One pass across every TU, driven from the disc's
own maps: the `SRCFILE.TXT` line map is the source-shape oracle, `MAIN.MAP`
and the census give names and layout. Per function, reason from the line map
and the compiler's own dumps; land the function when the rebuilt ELF is
byte-identical, and `ninja` is the only gate that says so. When it is not,
record the measured mechanism and the axes already tried in the ledger and
move on. There are no stall counters, no iteration budgets, no floor
verdicts and no permuter: the compiler is fixed, so a source shape that
produces those bytes exists. Diff counts are a signal for choosing the next
axis, never the objective.

**Converging a hard function.** Where the residual is whole-function register
allocation, reload or coloring rather than a local tie, drive the whole body
toward ROM's codegen instead of minimising the diff each step, and accept
diff-count regressions while you do: coloring classes snap into place in
groups. The levers (param memory-residency, alias-regime pairs, goto-CFG
tails, mode(TI) splits, census group-snap, …) and their *why* live in
`docs/COOKBOOK.md` §13.
```
